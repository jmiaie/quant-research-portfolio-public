# Research Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Reproduce and validate existing research

- Reproduce current flagship project outputs from clean environments
- Standardize benchmark definitions and result summaries
- Document assumptions, data lineage, and failure modes

## Phase 2 — Upgrade execution and statistical rigor

- Add formation/test separation and walk-forward workflows
- Improve transaction-cost, slippage, and execution modeling
- Expand uncertainty analysis, sensitivity analysis, and attribution

## Phase 3 — Expand into derivatives, portfolio risk, optimization, and financial ML

- Build the options-volatility-risk-lab modules
- Add reusable portfolio-risk and scenario-analysis tooling
- Extend the portfolio with self-contained financial-ML research that meets the same validation standards
